WHO WE ARE

A trusted partner for sourcing and supply.

ASANTESONG GLOBAL PROCUREMENT, TRADE & LOGISTICS LIMITED (ASGLOBE) is a Ghanaian company focused on procurement, product sourcing, commercial trade, wholesale supply and logistics coordination.

We work to connect organisations with suitable products, suppliers and supply-chain solutions, supporting their operational and commercial requirements through professional service and trusted business relationships.
