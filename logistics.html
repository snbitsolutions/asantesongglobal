Support for moving goods through the supply chain

Logistics coordination connected to procurement, sourcing and international trade enquiries.

Plan the supply journey

ASANTESONG GLOBAL PROCUREMENT, TRADE & LOGISTICS LIMITED coordinates logistics-related discussions as part of commercial sourcing and supply enquiries. We work with buyers and suppliers to understand shipment requirements and identify the arrangements that need to be confirmed.

Logistics enquiries can cover

  • Origin, destination and delivery requirements
  • Shipment timing and coordination needs
  • Import or export documentation requirements
  • Supplier and logistics-provider communication
  • Handling and other product-specific considerations

Transport, customs clearance, insurance, delivery dates and charges depend on the shipment and providers involved. Any service scope and arrangements should be confirmed for each transaction; statutory and customs requirements remain applicable.
