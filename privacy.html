Privacy Policy

How website enquiries and information are handled.

This policy describes how ASANTESONG GLOBAL PROCUREMENT, TRADE & LOGISTICS LIMITED (“ASANTESONGGLOBAL”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

Information you provide

If you submit a contact, quote or supplier enquiry, you may provide information such as your name, business contact details, organisation, product or service requirements, and the contents of your message. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand and follow up on your request, coordinate relevant sourcing or supply discussions, and communicate about our services. We may use contact details to reply by the method you provide.

Form and website providers

Website forms are submitted using Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through a form is processed by that provider to deliver the enquiry to us and may also be subject to the provider’s own privacy terms. This website also loads some fonts, icons and scripts from external providers; your browser may connect to those providers when loading the pages. Their handling of connection data is governed by their own policies.

Sharing and retention

We do not publish enquiry details on this website. We may share information with a supplier or service provider where needed to assess or respond to a request, or where disclosure is required by law. We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit information through the website and may contact us directly instead. To ask about information you have sent us or request an update, contact info@asantesongglobal.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
