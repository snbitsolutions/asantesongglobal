PROCUREMENT

Procurement support for organisations

Supplier identification, product sourcing and coordinated supply for business and institutional requirements.

A coordinated approach to buying

ASANTESONG GLOBAL PROCUREMENT, TRADE & LOGISTICS LIMITED helps organisations explore supply options and coordinate procurement enquiries. We work with buyers to understand requested products, specifications, quantities and delivery needs before coordinating with potential suppliers.

Procurement support may include

  • Clarifying product descriptions and buyer requirements
  • Identifying potential suppliers and sourcing options
  • Requesting and coordinating quotations
  • Supporting institutional and bulk supply enquiries
  • Coordinating with suppliers on availability and delivery requirements

Any proposed supply, pricing, availability and delivery arrangements are subject to confirmation for the specific enquiry.

How to get started

Send the product or service description, required specifications, quantity, destination and desired timeline. Our team can review the details and respond about next steps.
